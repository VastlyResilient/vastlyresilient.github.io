Stop 1 · Why questions win

The one who asks steers.

Human nature

People love talking about themselves.

Harvard, 2012: talking about ourselves lights up the brain's reward system. People even gave up money to do it.

Harvard, 2017: people who ask more follow-up questions are liked more.

The more they talk, the more they like you, and the more you learn.

Stop 6 · Can AI fix it?

Check the panel before you install.

Words or data on a screen or phone? Answering, scheduling, reminding: yes. Anything at the unit: no.

Repeatable, and mistakes catchable? A wrong reminder is cheap. A wrong $14,000 quote is not.

Can AI reach the data? That's the plug-in ladder, two cards ahead.

What fits in an HVAC shop

  • Answer after-hours calls and text back
  • Follow up on sent quotes
  • Ask for reviews after a job
  • Tune-up and maintenance reminders
  • Turn tech notes into clean invoices
  • Call summaries for the office
  • Dispatch suggestions and ETA texts
  • Draft quotes from past jobs; owner approves
  • Payment reminders; a person handles disputes
  • Rebate paperwork drafts (Energize CT); a person submits
  • Gas smell or CO calls: straight to a person or 911
  • Diagnosing equipment remotely
  • Final pricing decisions
  • Upset customers in a dispute

If a mistake could hurt someone or cost thousands, a person decides.
